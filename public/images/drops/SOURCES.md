# Drop artwork

Unmodified Warframe artwork © Digital Extremes, from [WFCD](https://github.com/WFCD/warframe-items) and [browse.wf](https://browse.wf/), resolved through [Public Export Plus](https://github.com/calamity-inc/warframe-public-export-plus). Uses the equipment catalog's pinned revisions.

[sources.json](sources.json) records URLs and SHA-256 hashes, including reused equipment images. Refresh with `npm run catalog:drop-art`; scene mappings: `scripts/drop-art-scenes.mjs`.

Artwork maps exact names or explicit recipe results; unmatched names show no substitute. Environment scenes are illustrative. [Data licenses](../SOURCES.md) do not transfer artwork ownership.
